# KZ-101 Only release tested tickets

Reporter: Aisyah (BA) · SDLC step: 9 Pre-release preparation, 10 Production deployment

## Story
As Infra, I want a ticket to reach Pending Production only when QA has resolved
ALL of its QAT sub-tickets, so that I never approve untested work.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A ticket moves to Pending Production only if every QAT sub-ticket is Resolved.
2. A ticket with no QAT sub-ticket cannot move to Pending Production.
3. The board shows why the move was refused.

## Answers from the interview (Module 2)
- "Testing is done" means every QAT sub-ticket is Resolved, not only the first.
- Any role can press the button; the rule is in the code, not in who clicks.
